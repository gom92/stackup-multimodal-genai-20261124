How it works — no keys, no accounts

  1. Text: your prompt goes to the free Pollinations text API, which writes a short story.
  2. Image: the same prompt goes to the free Pollinations image API, which draws the illustration.
  3. Speech: narration uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis — nothing leaves your device.

Prompts are sent to Pollinations only when you press “Tell me a story”. No sign-ups, no API keys, no uploads, no tracking. Speech recognition (the 🎤 button) also runs in your browser via the Web Speech API, where supported.
